# Batch 06: Remote-First Companies (Physical World / Operations / AI)

*Research date: 2026-04-10*
*Focus: Remote-first or fully distributed companies where AI is the lever, applied to hard physical-world or operational problems*
*Filter: Must be remote-first/fully remote. Series A-D, 50-500 people preferred. Not already evaluated.*
*Hard requirement: No hybrid, no on-site. Remote-first or bust.*
